# ProoFee: product vision

**Turn ideas into tokens. Turn progress into proof.**
ProoFee transforms speculative attention into verifiable progress.

This document describes where the product is going. What already works is listed in the
[README](../README.md#what-we-built) and [IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md);
anything below marked *planned* does not exist yet.

## Mission

Let people tokenize real ideas, dreams and goals, gather a community around them, and earn an
extra financial motivation for proven progress toward the result.

## Problem

Memecoin launchpads are very good at attention: launching is easy, trading is instant, communities
form fast. But the creator's reward is usually tied to hype alone. Fees can be taken on day one,
and nothing connects the money to delivering anything.

At the same time, many real ideas stall not for lack of interest but for lack of resources, public
accountability and motivation.

## Goal-backed tokens

ProoFee keeps the familiar launchpad mechanics but moves the focus from the meme to a real human
goal: build a product, shoot a film, record an album, open a business, write a book, learn a skill.

The creator publicly defines the goal, its success criteria and 3–5 milestones with weights and
deadlines. The goal specification is pinned on-chain by its sha256 hash, so it cannot be quietly
edited later. The audience follows the progress. The token is the social and economic layer around
the idea. It is **not** a promise of returns and **not** a share in a business.

## How rewards work

Trading activity can generate creator fees. In the verified testnet prototype:

- **20%** of the fees the goal receives is the immediate share, claimable at once;
- **80%** is split across milestones by weight and unlocks only when a milestone is accepted;
- the share of a rejected or missed milestone is **forfeited**: the creator can never claim it,
  including the same share of future fees. In this MVP it stays in the goal contract.

So the system rewards not only attracting attention but steady, verifiable work on the goal.
It does not hold back 100% of the reward: the 20% immediate share is available right away.

## Proof of Trust

Trust comes from public evidence, the possibility to dispute it, fixed rules and on-chain
consequences, not from the creator's reputation alone.

1. The creator submits a proof for a milestone: a link and the sha256 hash of its content.
2. A challenge window opens. Without a challenge, anyone can finalize the milestone after it.
3. With a Bond Treasury, a challenger posts a NEAR bond. The verifier first qualifies the
   challenge, then decides who is right.
4. Outcomes in the current MVP:
   - the challenge is not qualified → the bond is refunded;
   - the proof is invalid (the challenger was right) → the milestone is rejected and the bond is
     refunded, **with no extra reward**;
   - the proof is valid → the milestone is accepted and the bond is slashed to the buyback reserve;
   - the verifier does not decide within 24 h → anyone can accept the milestone by timeout.

Facts are checked by a **designated verifier account**. ProoFee is not a decentralized oracle or
DVM today. The idea is inspired by optimistic-oracle designs such as UMA, but there is no UMA
integration. Challenger rewards, staked independent arbitrators and stronger anti-griefing
protection are planned.

## The flywheel

```text
Ambitious, compelling idea
        ↓
Public goal + milestones + creator story
        ↓
Discovery, social sharing, community attention
        ↓
Participation and token trading activity
        ↓
Trading fees generated by real activity
        ↓
20% immediate / 80% milestone-linked creator rewards
        ↓
Motivation to deliver results and publish credible proofs
        ↓
Higher trust, engagement and further discovery
        ↺
```

Two loops are joined here, and this is what sets ProoFee apart from a plain memecoin launchpad:

1. **Social growth loop:** idea → story → discovery → community → activity → more discovery.
2. **Accountability loop:** activity → fees → milestone-linked incentives → proof →
   challenge and verification → trust → participation.

Economic hypothesis: if an idea draws interest, more people discover it, join its community and
trade its token; real trading volume generates fees; delivered milestones strengthen trust and
further participation. **None of this is guaranteed.** The token price can rise or fall, and
community and fee growth are not promised. Fees depend on trading activity and pool parameters,
not on the price alone.

In the future ProoFee may give more visibility to ambitious, meaningful goals (*planned*). Promotion
must not reward unrealistic promises by themselves: it should weigh the quality of the goal, clear
milestones, proofs, progress and community trust, with anti-spam, anti-sybil and moderation.

## Value to NEAR

- **New on-chain activity:** goal-backed token launches, swaps on Rhea DCL, LP fees, proof,
  challenge and claim transactions.
- **Potential new liquidity and audience:** creators, supporters and communities can come to NEAR
  for a specific goal, not only for trading. This is a potential, not a measured result.
- **Verifiable economics:** NEAR contracts enforce and record milestone rules, the fee split, bond
  outcomes and payouts.
- **Ecosystem integrations:** Rhea DCL is used in the testnet demo. NEAR Intents is a planned
  integration for chain-abstracted participation from other networks and assets, on supported
  routes and wallets.

ProoFee brings token-powered communities, goal-based incentives and verifiable human progress to
NEAR, with a path toward chain-abstracted participation through NEAR Intents.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Testnet MVP (current).** NEP-141 token, Rhea DCL trading, immutable keyless LP Locker,
Goal contract, 20/80 creator fee accounting, milestone proofs, free and bonded challenges, Bond
Treasury, on-chain claims, historical testnet evidence and the
[Judge Quickstart](JUDGE_QUICKSTART.md). A multi-user browser launch has not been confirmed by a
full public end-to-end run.

**Phase 2: Public launcher and low-friction creation (in progress).** Safe public web launch with a
NEAR wallet, storage and budget optimisation, fee transparency, a resilient orchestrator, protected
funding limits, factory and contract infrastructure, Trade/Proof/Challenge UX.

**Phase 3: Decentralized Proof of Trust (planned).** Independent staked verifiers, economic bonds,
voting or arbitration, slashing of dishonest participants, rewards for honest challengers,
reputation, spam protection and parallel challenges.

**Phase 4: Mainnet and NEAR Intents (planned).** Independent audit, security and economic review,
mainnet launch, NEAR Intents for cross-chain participation and simpler token purchases with
supported wallets and assets.

**Phase 5: Social discovery (planned).** Creator update feeds, follows, profiles, public progress
journals, milestone analytics, personalised recommendations, transparent promotion criteria and
reputation.

No dates or metrics are promised for these phases.

## Risks and honest limitations

- **Testnet only**, no independent audit; [AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md) is our own review.
- **One designated verifier.** A separate verifier address does not prove an independent owner;
  real goals need a multisig, DAO or staked verifier set (M-3).
- **One bonded challenge at a time per milestone.** An empty request can block real challengers
  until it expires (M-4, open).
- **No reward for a correct challenger** yet; the economics of checking proofs is an open product
  question.
- **The LP Locker is keyless and immutable.** Some failed or ambiguous operations can leave it stuck
  with no recovery path.
- **Market risk.** Token prices, trading volume and therefore fees are not guaranteed.
- On-chain records prove what was submitted and decided, not that a real-world fact is true. That
  still depends on the proof, the challenge window and the verifier.
